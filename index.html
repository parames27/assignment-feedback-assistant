Assignment Feedback Assistant

Get a structured, rubric-based feedback draft to support your own marking.

Rule-based demonstration: this tool counts rubric keywords in the answer. It does not use AI and cannot judge whether an answer is correct, well argued or original. All marks are suggestions that need teacher review.

Privacy: do not enter student names, ID numbers, emails, phone numbers, school or class details, or other identifying information. Use anonymous or made-up sample text. Everything is processed in your browser, and nothing is uploaded or saved.

Assignment details

One criterion per line (up to 8): Criterion name | points | keyword, keyword, keyword
Points are optional (if left out, marks are shared equally). If you give points, give them for every line and make them add up to the maximum score. If you leave out keywords, words from the criterion name are used.
